About the atlas

This interactive atlas presents 40 geological and heritage sites across South Africa. Select a symbol to explore its geological context, age, references and external resources.

References

W2015 — Gavin Whitfield, 50 Must-See Geological Sites in South Africa (2015).
VR1999 — M. J. Viljoen and W. U. Reimold, An Introduction to South Africa's Geological and Mining Heritage (1999).
NW2006 — Nick Norman and Gavin Whitfield, Geological Journeys (2006).

Map and icon credits
